Plan a visit September 11, 5:46 am

The steel

Window column 442, floors 91 to 94, Tower 2.

The piece came from the upper floors of the South Tower, bent as it was found. It is among the larger pieces of the towers released to any community, and it stands here as it was found, on a steel support, with nothing added to it.

The story

Nine years, three cities, and thousands of phone calls.

Jack Chandler asked for a piece of the towers in 2012. What arrived in 2015 was far larger than anyone had requested. It took three cities and six more years to stand it up.

  1. 2012

    The request. Jack Chandler applies to the Port Authority for a piece of World Trade Center steel for a memorial in Milton.

  2. June 2015

    The call. Three years later: a piece is available, much larger than the one asked for. The cities of Fife, Milton and Edgewood agree to take it together.

  3. 2015

    The drive. Truck driver Mike Earhart hauls the column from JFK Airport to Milton, at cost.

  4. November 2018

    The plan. Permit application filed with the City of Milton: a 35-foot memorial in a new plaza in Triangle Park, beside the Veterans Memorial. Sketches by Fife students shape the design.

  5. January 2019

    The notice. The city issues notice of a complete application. Site plan, landscape and lighting plans follow.

  6. September 11, 2021

    The dedication. Twenty years after the attacks, the steel is raised and the plaza is dedicated.

  7. September 11, 2026

    Twenty-five years. The remembrance at 5:46 am marks a quarter century.

Remembrance

Every September 11, at the minute the first plane hit.

8:46 am in New York is 5:46 am in Milton. VFW Posts 11401 and 2224 hold the ceremony at the plaza each year, before dawn. Everyone is welcome.
